Data sources and coverage

Finance publication, PhD, and employment records

Warning: All finance data are gathered by me on a best-effort basis. I have done my best to ensure accuracy as of November 15, 2026. Check all data points carefully.

The placement snapshot includes 4,711 faculty and placement-list records across 382 employing universities. We used LLMs to help collect records from university directories, faculty profiles, CVs, and selected PhD placement pages. Coverage varies by school, and the snapshot generally misses graduates who left academia. Publication counts come from a reconciled article catalog. The finance tenure charts show publications and citations at tenure grant. Dated promotions to associate professor serve as proxies when grant dates are unavailable. We exclude promotions that the evidence identifies as untenured.

Publication, PhD, and employment records in other fields

WARNING: Data for fields other than finance come from the Econ Atlas snapshot. Check all data points carefully.

The snapshot includes faculty, PhD, and appointment records for accounting, economics, marketing, management, and operations and information systems. The Econ Atlas paper index supplies publication records. The tenure pages use within-school promotions to associate professor as tenure proxies.

I do not maintain or control these data. Submit corrections or removal requests to the Econ Atlas maintainers.

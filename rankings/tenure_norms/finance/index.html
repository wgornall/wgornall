Finance tenure norms

These charts show faculty at the time they received tenure. When a grant date is unavailable, a dated promotion to associate professor serves as a proxy. We exclude promotions that the evidence identifies as untenured. The percentiles describe this group of successful cases. Warning: All finance data are gathered by me on a best-effort basis. I have done my best to ensure accuracy as of November 15, 2026. Check all data points carefully.

Publications and citations

These data are meant to help faculty, students, and prospective students start their research. Important decisions should be based on the actual data points after you have checked them, not the scores and certainly not the ranks. I am Will Gornall, a finance professor - I focused on the finance data and rankings and added other rankings on a best-effort basis using the econ-atlas data. I am also a Stanford GSB graduate, a UBC professor, and the director of UBC’s finance PhD program which creates biases, however, I have made what I see as neutral methodological choices and allowed you to customize them. This is an independent project, developed without institutional input from UBC.

- Will Gornall

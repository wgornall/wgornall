Georgia Institute of Technology and other schools’ promotion outcomes since 2015 (promotion to associate is a tenure proxy). Publications count the ten years before promotion through the promotion year; overlapping points are spread for visibility. Percentile guides are hidden. These data are meant to help faculty, students, and prospective students start their research. Important decisions should be based on the actual data points after you have checked them, not the scores and certainly not the ranks. I am Will Gornall, a finance professor - I focused on the finance data and rankings and added other rankings on a best-effort basis using the econ-atlas data. I am also a Stanford GSB graduate, a UBC professor, and the director of UBC’s finance PhD program which creates biases, however, I have made what I see as neutral methodological choices and allowed you to customize them. This is an independent project, developed without institutional input from UBC.

- Will Gornall
